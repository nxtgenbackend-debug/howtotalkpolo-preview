Privacy

This site keeps nothing about you

How to Talk Polo sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics. Almost every page loads nothing from other companies while you read.

The exception is Polo Posts that show live eBay listings. On those pages only, a script from eBay loads the current listings, and eBay may set cookies to credit a purchase to us. Each feed is labelled “Live on eBay”. eBay’s privacy policy applies to what it collects.

Pages with an eBay shelf (marked “On eBay now”) show listing photos straight from eBay’s servers, as eBay requires, so your browser fetches those images from eBay.

The merch shop loads its products and a small script from our store service, and remembers your cart in your own browser (not a cookie, and never sent anywhere until you check out). Payment happens on Stripe’s secure checkout page, and your order goes to our print partner, Printful, to be made and shipped. Their privacy policies apply to what they collect.

Some posts carry an advertiser’s banner, marked “Advertisement · affiliate link”. Its picture comes from the advertiser’s affiliate network (such as CJ or Awin), so your browser fetches it from their servers. Our Facebook and X links are plain links: nothing loads from either until you follow one.

Some posts include a YouTube video. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play; from then on YouTube’s privacy policy applies to that video.

When you follow a link to a shop, such as eBay, Etsy or Books-A-Million, that shop and the affiliate network it pays through (Awin or CJ) may set cookies to record that you came from here, so the commission can be credited. Their own privacy policies apply from that point.
