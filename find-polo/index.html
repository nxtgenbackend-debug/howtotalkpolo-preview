Polo near me: how to find a polo match to watch

There are polo clubs across the United States, and most of them welcome spectators. Few advertise, so here’s how to find one near you, and when to go.

Checked October 2026. Schedules change every season, so confirm dates on each club’s own site before you go.

How to find polo matches near you

  • Start with the USPA. The United States Polo Association lists its member clubs in its Find a Club search, and its tournament calendar at uspolo.org. If a club is near you, it’s there.
  • Follow the season. In the Midwest and the Northeast, polo is a summer sport. In Florida, home of the National Polo Center in Wellington, the big season runs through the winter. In colder states some clubs move to arena polo for the winter, in a smaller enclosed ring.
  • Check the club’s social media the week of a match. Rain cancels polo; wet fields can’t be played safely.
  • Look for the charity and cup days. Most clubs have one or two big public days a year. They’re the easiest introduction and the best people-watching.
