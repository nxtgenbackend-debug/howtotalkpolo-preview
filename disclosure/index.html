Disclosure

Some links here pay us a small commission

When you follow a link on this site to a shop such as eBay, Etsy or Books-A-Million and buy something, we may earn a commission. It costs you nothing extra, and it helps keep Don’s book and archive in print and online.

We are members of the eBay Partner Network and of CJ (Commission Junction), and we may join other programmes, such as Etsy’s through Awin, and clothing, wine and cigar retailers.

The bar above the header and the brand card near the foot of many pages are advertisements. Each is marked Sponsored, and we draw them ourselves: no advertising network loads anything onto this site while you read. Being an affiliate of a brand does not mean the brand endorses this site, or that we are its official partner.

Some Polo Posts end with a live eBay feed, marked “Live on eBay · affiliate links”. Those listings are chosen by eBay from a topic we set, so they change from day to day.

Wine, spirits and cigar picks are kept on a separate page for adults 21 and over, and never appear in ads elsewhere on the site.

Links that earn a commission are marked in the page code as sponsored, and any page that carries them says so near the top. We only list things we think a polo fan would actually enjoy.
