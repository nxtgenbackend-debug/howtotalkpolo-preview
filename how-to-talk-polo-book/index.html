The book

How to Talk Polo: A Fan’s Perspective, by Don Lubner

The polo announcer’s guide for the sideline: the rules, the words and the stories, with cartoons and caricatures to help them stick. Written by Don Lubner, colour man in the Milwaukee Polo Club booth, with a foreword by his booth partner, Dan Dance.
