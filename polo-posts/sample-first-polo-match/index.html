What to bring to your first polo match

By How to Talk Polo (sample) · Oct 1, 2026

Your first polo match is an afternoon outdoors on a very large lawn. Pack for that, and everything else takes care of itself. Here is what to bring.

Something to sit on

The sideline is grass, and by the third chukker it can be damp grass. A blanket is the classic; a pair of chairs is kinder on the knees.
