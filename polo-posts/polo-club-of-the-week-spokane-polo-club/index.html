Polo Club of the Week - Spokane Polo Club

By Charles Lubner · Jun 1, 2026

The "Polo Club of the Week" is Spokane Polo Club, based in Spokane, WA.

In eastern Washington’s open country, Spokane Polo Club brings polo to the Inland Northwest. The SPC also runs a polo school for beginners.

The Spokane season runs June - September and features Peter Dix Memorial Tournament / USPA Pacific Northwest Circuit Players Cup.

View the club’s playing schedule at https://www.spokanepoloclub.org.
