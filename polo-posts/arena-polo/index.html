Arena Polo

By Charles Lubner · Nov 4, 2026

Arena polo is a great way to start watching polo. First of all, it's played on a smaller field, in this case a dirt arena. There's three players per team instead of four, and the action is closer to the spectators. Many polo players begin playing arena polo because of lower startup costs and arenas can fit in areas of the city where a grass polo field would make land costs prohibitive.

One of the most celebrated arena polo venues is in Philadelphia. Work to Ride has been featured on 60 Minutes and other news programs. It's a program designed to train inner city youth how to play polo - for free - in exchange for mucking the stables and doing other upkeep around the barn.

Your local college or university may have an arena polo program. Click here to search: https://www.uspolo.org/clubs/
