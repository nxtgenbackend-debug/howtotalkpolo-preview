Book review: Crossing the Line by Kareem Rosser

By Charles Lubner · Oct 23, 2026

The book

Crossing the Line

by Kareem Rosser

Get it at Books-A-Million

Kareem Rosser's Crossing the Line is a powerful and inspiring memoir about perseverance, opportunity, and the transformative power of polo. Growing up in West Philadelphia, Rosser discovers an unlikely path from the challenges of urban life to the competitive world of equestrian sports.

Through his experiences with the Work to Ride program, Rosser recounts how polo provided not only an athletic outlet but also discipline, friendship, and a vision for a different future. His journey challenges traditional perceptions of who belongs in the sport while celebrating the mentors and teammates who helped him succeed.

From Philadelphia's pavement to polo's prestigious playing fields, Rosser's poignant personal journey proves that passion, persistence, and perseverance can prevail.

More than a sports memoir, Crossing the Line is a compelling story of brotherhood, resilience, and breaking barriers. It's an excellent read for polo enthusiasts and anyone who appreciates an extraordinary underdog story.
